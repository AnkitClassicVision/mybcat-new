# MyBCAT Website Product Context

## Product

MyBCAT operates and measures patient-access work for optometry groups. The website has two distinct jobs:

1. Help optometry owners and operators with 3 or more locations evaluate MyBCAT services.
2. Give owners of 1 to 3-location Southeast optometry practices a separate, confidential way to express transition interest.

The service funnel is primary. Practice transitions are a separate secondary funnel and must never enroll a seller in service marketing without explicit consent.

## Users

- Primary: optometry owners and operators responsible for patient access across 3 or more locations.
- Secondary: owner-led optometry practices in GA, FL, AL, TN, SC, and NC considering a transition.
- Internal: Ankit as qualification owner and the designated operator as follow-up owner.
- Existing healthcare vertical visitors remain supported by their dedicated pages.

## Voice and personality

Confident, operational, precise, and discreet. Explain what MyBCAT runs and how the result is measured. Avoid hype, generic outsourcing language, unsupported financial claims, and artificial urgency.

## Design principles

1. Optometry first on the homepage and primary service journey.
2. One dominant intent, one call to action, and one destination per priority page.
3. Preserve the existing titanium, teal, white-space-led visual system.
4. Make proof and operating clarity more prominent than decoration.
5. Keep the service and acquisition funnels visually and behaviorally separate.
6. Design mobile-first for fast, accessible form completion.

## Anti-references

- Generic healthcare-BPO homepages that could describe any vendor.
- AI-generic SaaS card grids with no operational evidence.
- Valuation or transaction claims that lack a named, current source.
- Forms that mix seller interest with service marketing.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All forms need explicit labels, keyboard operation, visible focus, concise error messages, live status announcements, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion-safe behavior.

## Release boundary

Local implementation and internal review are allowed. Publishing, GTM changes, CRM writes, scheduling changes, production environment changes, and external follow-up require action-time approval. Twenty is an intended CRM destination only until its separate canonical gate passes.

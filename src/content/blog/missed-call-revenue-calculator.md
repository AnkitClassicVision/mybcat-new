Most groups dramatically underestimate this number. Use the calculator below to see your actual exposure.

### Quick Estimate Formula

If you want a quick calculation:

```
Annual Revenue Loss =
  (Locations × Daily Calls × Miss Rate × 365 × Conversion Rate × Patient Value)
```

### Example Calculation

**10-location optometry group:**
- 10 locations
- 40 calls per day per location
- 29% miss rate (industry average)
- 365 days
- 25% would have converted
- $1,400 first-year patient value

**Calculation:**
```
10 × 40 × 0.29 × 365 × 0.25 × $1,400 = $1,489,600
```

**That's nearly $1.5 million in annual revenue loss from missed calls alone.**

## Understanding the Variables

### Daily Call Volume

Average inbound calls per location. If unsure, check your phone system reports or estimate:

| Practice Type | Typical Daily Calls |
|---------------|-------------------|
| General Optometry | 30-50 |
| Ophthalmology | 40-70 |
| Dental | 50-80 |
| Veterinary | 35-60 |

### Miss Rate

Percentage of calls that go unanswered or to voicemail.

| Scenario | Typical Miss Rate |
|----------|------------------|
| Well-staffed, single location | 15-20% |
| Average multi-location | 25-35% |
| Understaffed or high-growth | 35-50% |
| Peak hours only | 40-70% |

### Conversion Rate

What percentage of answered calls result in scheduled appointments?

| Call Type | Typical Conversion |
|-----------|-------------------|
| New patient inquiry | 60-80% |
| Existing patient scheduling | 85-95% |
| Blended average | 70-80% |

### Patient Value

First-year revenue from a new patient (or lifetime value for longer calculation):

| Specialty | First-Year Value | Lifetime Value |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------|
| Optometry | $400-800 | $1,400-2,800 |
| Dental | $500-1,200 | $3,000-8,000 |
| Ophthalmology | $800-2,000 | $4,000-12,000 |
| Veterinary | $600-1,500 | $3,500-10,000 |

## What Your Number Means

### Under $100K
Your intake is performing well, or you have low volume. Focus on optimization rather than overhaul.

### $100K - $500K
Significant opportunity. A dedicated intake solution would likely pay for itself within 6-12 months.

### $500K - $1M
Major revenue leak. This should be a top priority. Every month of delay costs $40-80K.

### Over $1M
Critical issue. Your intake infrastructure isn't scaled for your organization. Immediate action recommended.

## Beyond the Calculator

This calculator captures direct missed call losses. It doesn't account for:

- **Reputation damage** from frustrated callers
- **Marketing waste** on leads that never connect
- **Staff burnout** from constant phone pressure
- **Competitive loss** to practices with better access
- **PE multiple impact** on enterprise value

The true cost is often 1.5-2x the calculated number.

## Next Steps Based on Your Results

### If loss is under $100K:
- Implement call tracking if you haven't already
- Review scripts for conversion optimization
- Consider AI-assisted overflow during peaks

### If loss is $100K-$500K:
- Audit miss rates by location and time
- Evaluate virtual front desk solutions
- Calculate ROI on centralized intake

### If loss is over $500K:
- This is an urgent operational issue
- Begin vendor evaluation immediately
- Consider interim solutions while building long-term fix

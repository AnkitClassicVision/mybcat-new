Eye care professionals consistently see recurring patients. As a [managed healthcare intake partner](https://mybcat.com/industries/optometry/), we understand that the services they provide, paired with efficient back-office functions, allow them to create meaningful and productive relationships with their clients.

Here at My Business Care Team, we aim to make it easier to foster better relationships with your optometry clients through our [eye care call support](https://mybcat.com/blog/). Let’s dive deeper into several functions needed to realize this goal.

- **Open and Effective Communication**

An optometrist's office should practice openly communicating with clients. Clients depend on healthcare professionals for their health. It is crucial that patients receive relevant information about their health state.

Furthermore, having professionals to help with [optometrist call management](https://mybcat.com/about/) allows for accessible communication lines. Clients can trust you more when they know they can reach your office as needed.

- **Urgency in Addressing Problems**

Fostering [positive relationships](https://web.archive.org/web/20260411011111/https://trainingmag.com/8-tips-for-developing-positive-relationships/) with clients becomes more feasible when your office takes every step to solve their problems and concerns. You can take in concerns more efficiently with [optometrist inbound call services](https://mybcat.com/about/), allowing you to address them as soon as possible.

- **Building Rapport**

The relationships you can have with your clients can depend on how well your client-facing employees can build rapport. We all gravitate to service providers who make us feel like we matter. Crafting harmonious interactions helps your clients be more comfortable. Building rapport will help you take care of the relationships you create with your optometry clients.

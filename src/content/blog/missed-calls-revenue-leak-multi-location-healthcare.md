Multi-location healthcare groups lose an average of $1.2 million annually to missed calls. This isn't speculation—it's math.

## The Hidden Revenue Hemorrhage

Every unanswered call represents more than an inconvenience. For multi-location healthcare groups—DSOs, optometry networks, veterinary chains—it's a compounding revenue leak that most organizations never quantify.

The typical 10-location group loses approximately **$1.2 million annually** to missed call opportunities. Here's how it breaks down.

## The Numbers Don't Lie

### Call Volume Reality

- The average healthcare practice misses **29% of inbound calls**
- Dental practices experience worse results—up to **68% during peak hours**
- **85% of callers won't try calling back** after an initial unanswered attempt
- **67% of healthcare consumers will call a competitor** if they don't reach their primary practice quickly

### Per-Miss Cost by Specialty

The financial impact varies significantly by specialty:

| Specialty | Cost Per Missed Call |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Dental | $735–$3,500 |
| Optometry | $1,400–$2,800 |
| Veterinary | $1,750–$3,500 |

These figures account for patient lifetime value, family referral multipliers, and emergency call premiums.

## The PE-Backed Group Multiplier

For private equity-backed groups, recovered revenue doesn't just hit the P&L—it multiplies through EBITDA calculations.

At a typical 6–12x multiple, **$100K in recovered revenue can add $600K–$1.2M to enterprise valuation**.

This transforms call capture from an operational metric into a strategic imperative.

## Why Traditional Solutions Fail

Simply hiring more front desk staff doesn't solve the problem:

1. **Training time**: New hires take 60-90 days to reach competency
2. **Turnover**: Front desk turnover in healthcare averages 40%+ annually
3. **Coverage gaps**: Lunch breaks, PTO, and sick days create predictable vulnerability windows
4. **Scale limitations**: You can't staff for peak volume without overstaffing for average volume

## The Systematic Solution

Effective multi-location call capture requires:

- **Centralized overflow handling** across all locations
- **After-hours coverage** for the 40% of calls that come outside business hours
- **Real-time dashboards** with location-level visibility
- **PM software integration** for immediate appointment booking

## Calculate Your Leak

Use this formula to estimate your organization's annual missed call revenue loss:

```
(Daily call volume × Miss rate × 365 days × Average patient value) = Annual loss
```

For a 10-location group:
- 50 calls/day/location = 500 total
- 29% miss rate = 145 missed daily
- 365 days = 52,925 missed annually
- $25 conversion rate × $400 avg first-year value = $528K minimum

Factor in lifetime value multipliers and the number exceeds $1.2M quickly.

## Next Steps

1. Audit your current miss rate across locations
2. Calculate the revenue impact using real patient values
3. Evaluate centralized intake solutions designed for multi-location scale

The leak won't fix itself. Every day of inaction costs money.

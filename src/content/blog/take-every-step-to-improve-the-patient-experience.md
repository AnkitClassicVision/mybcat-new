Running an eye clinic can lead to hectic moments. Amid this hectic environment, operators must uphold the experience they create for their clients. Here at My Business Care Team, we make sure that every optometrist patient has a pleasant experience through our [specialized optometry patient intake services](https://mybcat.com/about/). Let's talk about some factors needed to improve the client experience.

- **Keep Communication Lines Active and Accessible**

Booking an [optometrist appointment](https://mybcat.com/blog/) should be a smooth experience for clients. This is only possible when your clinic maintains an active and accessible communication line.

Utilizing a [vision care call center](https://mybcat.com/solutions/medical-virtual-assistant/) to communicate with patients allows clients to reach you with maximum convenience. Outsourcing this crucial task also takes a lot off of your plate, giving you the energy to focus on your clients. For practices managing [multiple locations, centralized intake solutions](https://mybcat.com/blog/multi-location-healthcare-intake-guide/) become essential for maintaining consistent service.

- **People Skills**

Applying people skills in every [client interaction](https://ca.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/customer-interactions) gives clients assurance that they are dealing with professionals. Transactions also become seamless when you know how to deal with different types of people. With [optometry virtual receptionists](https://mybcat.com/industries/optometry/), you can apply these soft skills to create a more welcoming experience for your patients.

- **Learn from Your Patients**

Even an eye clinic won't be immune from client feedback. Like all healthcare operations, the well-being of every patient should come first. Take the time to listen to what your clients are saying about your operations. Utilize [call support](https://mybcat.com/blog/the-role-of-call-support-in-patient-satisfaction/) functions for feedback collection. Making the right adjustments to your operations should hinge on constructive feedback from the people you serve.

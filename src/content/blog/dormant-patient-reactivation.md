## Understanding Dormant Patients

Dormant patients are those who have not visited your practice in a significant amount of time. Reactivating them is essential for maintaining a healthy revenue stream and ensuring patient care continuity. In this playbook, we will outline actionable steps to engage these patients effectively over the next 30 days.

## Week 1: Analyze Your Patient Data

Start by analyzing your patient database to identify dormant patients. Look for those who haven't visited in the last 12 months. Create a list and categorize them based on demographics, treatment history, and previous engagement. This will help tailor your reactivation strategies.

### Checklist:
- Extract a list of dormant patients from your database.
- Segment them based on demographics (age, treatment type, etc.).
- Identify patterns in their previous visits.

## Week 2: Develop Targeted Messaging

Craft personalized messages that resonate with your dormant patients. Highlight the importance of regular check-ups and any new services or technologies your practice offers. Consider sending emails, postcards, or even making phone calls.

### Example Message:
"We miss you at [Practice Name]! It's been a while since your last visit. Let us help you get back on track with your health. Schedule an appointment today and discover our new services!"

## Week 3: Implement a Multi-Channel Approach

Leverage multiple channels to reach out to your dormant patients. Use email campaigns, social media posts, and even SMS reminders. The goal is to ensure that your message reaches them effectively.

### Action Steps:
- Design an email campaign featuring reactivation offers.
- Post engaging content on social media about the benefits of returning.
- Use SMS to send appointment reminders or special promotions.

## Week 4: Monitor and Adjust Your Strategy

After implementing your reactivation efforts, monitor the results closely. Track appointment bookings from dormant patients and assess the effectiveness of your messaging. Adjust your strategies based on what works best.

### Metrics to Monitor:
- Number of appointments booked by dormant patients.
- Engagement rates of email and SMS campaigns.
- Feedback from patients who return.

## Conclusion

Reactivating dormant patients is a crucial aspect of maintaining your practice's revenue and ensuring ongoing patient care. By following this 30-day playbook, you can effectively engage these patients and encourage them to return.

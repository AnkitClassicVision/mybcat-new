mybcat.com Website Restructuring Blueprint
Executive Summary (10 Key Findings)

mybcat.com has strong positioning as a "managed patient acquisition system" (not hourly VA provider), but the current site architecture is chaotic with 50+ orphaned legacy pages creating duplicate content issues and diluted SEO authority.
Primary competitors Hello Rache ($9.50/hr) and HireTeem ($11/hr) position as hourly VA staffing services, while mybcat's outcome-based model ("we sell results, not hours") is a meaningful differentiator that requires clearer site communication.
Optometry vertical dominance - mybcat has deep optometry expertise but underserves expansion verticals (dental, veterinary, medspa, urgent care, ENT) with minimal dedicated content.
Critical keyword opportunities exist in niche vertical queries ("optometry answering service," "dental practice answering service") with medium competition, while head terms ("medical virtual assistant services") are highly competitive.
SERP analysis reveals listicle content dominates for "best" queries, specialty landing pages win for vertical queries, and problem-aware content ("reduce no-shows") favors numbered tip formats.
Astro framework is recommended for the rebuild - zero JavaScript by default Astro delivers optimal Core Web Vitals (LCP <2.5s, CLS <0.1) with excellent MDX content authoring for the code-first workflow.
FAQ schema rich results are restricted to government/health authority sites only google (Google 2023 policy), but implementing FAQPage schema still helps AI understanding and extraction. Google
AI Overview optimization requires 40-60 word "answer capsules" at the top of each section, Frase Q&A heading structure, and ranking in top 10 organically (52% of AI citations come from top 10 results).
Content gaps include missing comparison pages vs competitors, no transparent pricing, limited case studies, no ROI calculator, and insufficient trust signals (certifications, compliance badges).
30-day MVP should prioritize homepage rebuild, 3 core service pages, 5 industry verticals (optometry, dental, veterinary, medspa, dermatology), and comparison content vs Hello Rache/HireTeem.

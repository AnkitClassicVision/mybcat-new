## Evidence set

1. [VA Health Care: Clinical Contact Center Modernization](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-22-104620)
   - Publisher: U.S. Government Accountability Office
   - Accessed: 2026-08-11
   - Bounded evidence: GAO found that standardized contact-center services need shared implementation schedules, defined performance metrics and targets, clear assessment roles, and technology data such as call-queue information.
   - Supports: centralized access requires common workflows, owners, and comparable measures
2. [Contact Center Technologies](https://digital.gov/resources/contact-center-guidelines/contact-center-technologies/)
   - Publisher: Digital.gov
   - Accessed: 2026-08-11
   - Bounded evidence: Digital.gov recommends observing incoming calls, queued calls, abandoned calls, and talk time so managers can recognize routing and capacity problems and take corrective action.
   - Supports: routing gaps should be diagnosed from queue and outcome data
3. [Open Access Scheduling for Routine and Urgent Appointments](https://www.ahrq.gov/cahps/quality-improvement/improvement-guide/6-strategies-for-improving/access/strategy6a-openaccess.html)
   - Publisher: Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality
   - Accessed: 2026-08-11
   - Bounded evidence: AHRQ's implementation guidance emphasizes measuring supply and demand, simplifying appointment types, creating contingency plans, tracking requests prospectively, and monitoring appointment availability.
   - Supports: access operations need standardized definitions, demand data, and contingency rules
4. [May Providers Leave Messages for Patients?](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/faq/198/may-health-care-providers-leave-messages/index.html)
   - Publisher: U.S. Department of Health and Human Services
   - Accessed: 2026-08-11
   - Bounded evidence: HHS permits patient communications by phone or mail but advises limiting message content, using professional judgment, and honoring reasonable confidential-communication requests.
   - Supports: contact workflows need minimum-necessary message rules and preference handling
5. [VA Appointment Wait Times and Scheduling Oversight](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-13-130)
   - Publisher: U.S. Government Accountability Office
   - Accessed: 2026-08-11
   - Bounded evidence: GAO found that wait-time measures depend on schedulers recording the desired date consistently, showing why metric definitions and training affect reporting reliability.
   - Supports: location scorecards require consistent event definitions and data entry

## Evidence set

1. [Doctors of optometry offer transition tips, whether buying or selling](https://www.aoa.org/news/practice-management/perfect-your-practice/business-transition-tips-for-buying-or-selling)
   - Publisher: American Optometric Association
   - Accessed: 2026-08-11
   - Bounded evidence: AOA's optometrist interviews describe transition planning as a multi-year process shaped by owner goals, patient continuity, buyer fit, professional advice, and the owner's desired post-sale role.
   - Supports: transition choices should begin with owner goals and continuity
2. [Ethical Considerations when Selling a Practice](https://www.aoa.org/AOA/Documents/Education/Education_PDFs/Case%20Study%2016-Selling%20a%20Practice.pdf)
   - Publisher: American Optometric Association
   - Accessed: 2026-08-11
   - Bounded evidence: AOA's ethics case focuses on advance planning and the ethical handling of patient notification and continuity when ownership changes.
   - Supports: patient communication and continuity require deliberate transition planning
3. [North Carolina Optometry Rules: Ceasing Practice](https://ncrules.state.nc.us/ncac/title%2021%20-%20occupational%20licensing%20boards%20and%20commissions/chapter%2042%20-%20optometry/subchapter%20b/subchapter%20b%20rules.pdf)
   - Publisher: North Carolina Office of Administrative Hearings
   - Accessed: 2026-08-11
   - Bounded evidence: North Carolina's optometry rule requires notice to the Board, notice to affected patients, proof of notice, and continued custody or transfer arrangements for records when a location ceases practice.
   - Supports: practice cessation requires state-specific patient notice and record custody steps
4. [Your Medical Records](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-individuals/medical-records/index.html)
   - Publisher: U.S. Department of Health and Human Services
   - Accessed: 2026-08-11
   - Bounded evidence: HHS explains that patients generally have rights to inspect and receive copies of covered medical and billing records, while transfers to another provider depend on treatment, payment, or permission.
   - Supports: patient access and authorized record transfer remain continuity requirements
5. [Close or Sell Your Business](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/manage-your-business/close-or-sell-your-business)
   - Publisher: U.S. Small Business Administration
   - Accessed: 2026-08-11
   - Bounded evidence: SBA recommends a thorough ownership-transfer plan, qualified professional advice, a valuation before marketing, review of tangible and intangible assets, and a comprehensive sales agreement.
   - Supports: sale readiness requires planning, records, valuation, and professional review

## Evidence set

1. [Close or Sell Your Business](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/manage-your-business/close-or-sell-your-business)
   - Publisher: U.S. Small Business Administration
   - Accessed: 2026-08-11
   - Bounded evidence: SBA recommends a thorough ownership-transfer plan, qualified professional advice, a valuation before marketing, review of tangible and intangible assets, and a comprehensive sales agreement.
   - Supports: sale readiness requires planning, records, valuation, and professional review
2. [Sale of a Business](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/sale-of-a-business)
   - Publisher: Internal Revenue Service
   - Accessed: 2026-08-11
   - Bounded evidence: IRS guidance explains that a business sale commonly involves separate assets whose classifications and allocated consideration affect gain or loss treatment.
   - Supports: a practice sale should not be reduced to one headline number
3. [Instructions for Form 8594](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8594)
   - Publisher: Internal Revenue Service
   - Accessed: 2026-08-11
   - Bounded evidence: IRS instructions state that qualifying asset sales can require both purchaser and seller to report the transaction and its asset allocation on Form 8594.
   - Supports: asset allocation is a coordinated buyer-seller tax workstream
4. [Your Medical Records](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-individuals/medical-records/index.html)
   - Publisher: U.S. Department of Health and Human Services
   - Accessed: 2026-08-11
   - Bounded evidence: HHS explains that patients generally have rights to inspect and receive copies of covered medical and billing records, while transfers to another provider depend on treatment, payment, or permission.
   - Supports: patient access and authorized record transfer remain continuity requirements
5. [Business Valuation Guidelines](https://www.irs.gov/irm/part4/irm_04-048-004)
   - Publisher: Internal Revenue Service
   - Accessed: 2026-08-11
   - Bounded evidence: IRS valuation guidance calls for a defined purpose, valuation date, assumptions, limitations, information sources, relevant business analysis, documented workpapers, and professional judgment across asset, market, and income approaches.
   - Supports: valuation depends on scope, evidence, assumptions, and method selection

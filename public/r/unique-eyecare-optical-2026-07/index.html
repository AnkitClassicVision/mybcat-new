The traffic is real. The traffic is wrong.

The growth is genuine, but it does not book eye exams in Houston. One blog post carries the site.

AI readiness — the bright spot

Nearly every query the XDEMVY article wins sits in an AI Overview-heavy results page, and the article holds rank 1 for several. That is real evidence the content is citation-worthy to answer engines on medical topics.

AI readiness — the gap

None of it helps a Houston patient. The local business entity is still fuzzy to machines: broken and missing schema, no author or clinical reviewer on health posts, the http/https split unresolved, and an empty llms.txt. Answer engines cite the drug article, not “eye doctor near me.”

Page experience — re-tested live July 23 (DataForSEO Lighthouse)

Speed improved: desktop LCP now passes at 1.9s and mobile performance rose to 79. But mobile accessibility is unchanged at 43 and mobile LCP still fails the 2.5s target, both traceable to the homepage’s 62 images with 59 missing alt text.

Priority fix list

The P0 list from May 6 is unchanged and now overdue. The new priority: stop the duplicate that is actively ranking, then convert the traffic already earned.

P0

Force HTTP to HTTPS with 301 redirects

The http:// homepage is confirmed indexed and ranking as a duplicate. This is now proven signal loss, not a theoretical risk.

High impact
P0

Add self-referencing canonicals to all 14 uncovered pages

Only the homepage has one today. Every service and blog page still needs a clean HTTPS canonical.

High impact
P0

Write the 8 missing service meta descriptions and fix short titles

Pages like avulux (6 characters) and specialty contacts (14) still have generic titles and blank descriptions.

High impact
P0

Fix or redirect /frames.html and /contact.html

frames.html is still 404 and contact.html has newly broken since May. Restore or 301 both.

High impact
P1

Repair the invalid schema and add service/article markup

Two JSON-LD blocks fail to parse, the homepage schema still uses an http:// URL, and service and blog pages have none.

High impact
P1

Convert the XDEMVY traffic you already have

Add author, clinical reviewer, Article schema, and a Houston appointment CTA to the post pulling 207 visits a month.

High impact
P1

Rebuild priority local service pages

Local, patient-useful copy, doctor expertise, FAQs, insurance context, and appointment CTAs for exams, dry eye, pediatric, myopia, and contacts.

High impact

Earn real local editorial links (chambers, Rice Village / West University press, partners). Clean up the spam directory links.
